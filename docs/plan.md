### 2b · The 3D room (Arnav said go, 2026-10-19; broken down below, **awaiting approval**)

Brain dive in three.js, the room and its props, the holotable (Earth intro, icons, windows) and every prop and holotable animation, Mumbai-time day/night, HUD hotspot list, performance gate. GSAP arrives here, lazy on `/workshop` (Lenis is already in). Meshy/Tripo/Mixamo accounts are only needed for hero props and the avatar: every prop first ships as procedural geometry so nothing blocks, and a Meshy swap is a later, separate go (asked for at the end of the task that needs it).

**Look and feel (design brief, from the SPEC plus awesome-design-md)**
- _The room_: stylised realism, not low-poly. A graphite workshop at night in Mumbai; light is the only decoration. Tungsten practicals (`--time`) warm the walls and desks, cyan (`--holo`) belongs to holograms only, red (`--action`) to the neon sign and primary actions. Colours in 3D come from the same tokens (read from CSS at runtime, so Night and Day stay one source of truth). No Marvel names or likeness; the armour wall is four original suits.
- _The HUD over the room_ (after **SpaceX**, awesome-design-md): the interface disappears behind the scene. Text sits straight on the 3D with no panels or cards: mono (JetBrains) uppercase labels with wide tracking, one ghost pill button style (translucent fill, 1px hairline), no shadows. Hotspot label `[ E ] OPEN HOLOTABLE`, zone list bottom-left, back chip top-left, quality and sound in Settings.
- _Holo-windows and the holotable desktop_ (after **Linear's** density and hover states, in cyan): hairline `--line` borders, a dark translucent surface with scanlines only inside hologram surfaces, 12 px radius, mono titles, one accent per panel (cyan only, never beside tungsten).
- _Motion_: camera moves use `--ease-cam` (1.4–2.2 s), reveals `--ease-out`; reduced motion = cuts and fades, no shake, no flicker. One signature moment per layer: the Earth-to-Mumbai holotable intro; everything else stays calm.
- _Never_: a generic spinner, a prop that could sit in anyone's portfolio, anything 3D-only (every hotspot also reachable from the HUD list and from the 2D site).

**Scope decision to confirm:** 2b builds every prop with its look, hover, focus, camera shot and its animation, and opens the matching content from `content/` (a plaque or a holo-window). The deep interactions stay in Phase 4 as the SPEC's gate says: Retro TV timeline by channel, transformer Q&A, notebook inbox write, chalkboard moderation, game leaderboard, Cricket World and Project World (Phase 3).
